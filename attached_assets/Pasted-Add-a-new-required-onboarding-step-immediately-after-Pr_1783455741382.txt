Add a new required onboarding step immediately after “Pricing Rules” called “Invoice Formatting.”

This step must appear above the divider in the Agent Management sidebar, before Widget Settings, Test Agent, Dashboard, Leads Inbox, and Billing unlock. Users must complete Business Profile, Services, Pricing Rules, and Invoice Formatting before anything below the divider becomes available.

Purpose:
The business owner chooses how their BDA will format customer-facing invoice/estimate previews. The invoice must dynamically adapt to each business using that business’s profile, company name, industry, services, pricing rules, policies, terms, cancellation policy, and customer/project details.

Create 4 selectable invoice templates:

1. Simple Summary Invoice
- Shorter format
- Clean itemized pricing
- Brief policy agreement sentence at the bottom

2. Modern Estimate Card
- Shorter format
- Slightly more visual/card-style layout
- Brief policy agreement sentence at the bottom

3. Detailed Service Agreement Invoice
- Same general structure as Template 1
- Includes full written terms, cancellation policy, payment terms, estimate disclaimer, and customer acceptance language

4. Detailed Professional Proposal
- Same general structure as Template 2
- More polished proposal-style layout
- Includes full written terms, cancellation policy, payment terms, estimate disclaimer, and customer acceptance language

Requirements:
- Show all 4 templates as preview cards
- Allow the user to select one default invoice format
- Store selected_invoice_template on the business record or invoice_settings table
- Add an invoice_settings table if needed
- Include editable fields for:
  - Cancellation policy
  - Payment terms
  - Estimate disclaimer
  - Terms and conditions
  - Acceptance language
  - Deposit requirements
  - Invoice footer note
- Pre-fill reasonable default language based on local service businesses
- Use the actual business name from Business Profile
- Use actual services from the Services step
- Use actual pricing rules from Pricing Rules
- Make previews look like real invoices, not placeholders
- The preview should update when company name, service, price, tax, cancellation policy, or terms change
- Add “Save Invoice Format & Continue”
- Once saved, mark Invoice Formatting as completed in onboarding progress

AI integration:
When the BDA generates an estimate, it should use the selected invoice template to structure the invoice preview. The AI should return structured invoice data, but the frontend should render it using the selected template.

Do not hardcode company names like ABC Services except as fallback demo data. All invoice previews should adapt dynamically to the logged-in business profile.
