--------------------------------------------------
AI ESTIMATING LOGIC UPDATE
--------------------------------------------------

When generating estimates:

1. First check if the service has its own pricing values.
2. If missing, use company defaults.
3. Calculate labor using:

crew size × labor hours × billable labor rate

4. Calculate internal profitability using:

crew size × labor hours × employee wage

5. Include:
- service-specific pricing factors
- duration
- crew size
- inspection requirements
- typical job ranges

The goal is that every service a company offers can have its own unique estimating logic.

Do not remove existing functionality. Update the existing UI and database structure cleanly.
