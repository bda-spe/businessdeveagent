Update the embedded widget quote flow and quote output enforcement.

1. Required service address
Every embedded widget quote request must require the customer to enter the service address before a quote can be generated.

Add required fields:
- Street address
- City
- State
- ZIP code

The widget should not generate a quote until service address is provided.

Include the service address in:
- AI quote context
- Quote summary
- Quote PDF
- Business owner email
- Customer quote email
- Saved lead/quote record

2. Dynamic customer budget ranges
The widget budget range options must be dynamic per business.

Do not hardcode generic options like:
- $0-$250
- $500-$1,000
- $5,000+

Instead, generate ranges using the business’s saved pricing profile:
- typical_low_end_job_cost
- average_job_cost
- typical_high_end_job_cost
- minimum_job_charge

Example:
If low-end job cost is $35 and high-end job cost is $125, show realistic ranges like:
- Under $50
- $50-$75
- $75-$100
- $100-$125
- $125+
- Not sure

If low/high costs are missing, fallback to industry defaults, but log that fallback was used.

3. Enforce selected quote format
The quote generated in the widget must use the selected Quote Formatting template saved by the business.

Do not use a generic quote layout if the business has selected a quote format.

When generating and rendering quotes:
- Fetch selected_quote_template from quote/invoice settings
- Use that template for widget quote preview
- Use that template for quote PDF
- Use that template for quote email
- Use enabled quote sections/check boxes from quote settings
- Apply business-specific quote language, policies, disclaimers, and acceptance language

4. Estimate disclaimer language
All quote outputs must clearly state that the generated quote is a preliminary estimate, not a final service agreement.

Use language similar to:
“This quote is a preliminary estimate based on the information provided. It is not a final service agreement. Final pricing may change after review, inspection, measurement, material confirmation, or changes to project scope.”

Add this disclaimer to:
- Widget quote result
- Quote PDF
- Quote summary email
- Business owner copied email
- Test Agent quote output

5. Terminology
Continue using “Quote” as the main user-facing term, but the explanatory disclaimer may use “preliminary estimate” to clarify that pricing is not final.

Avoid calling the output an invoice or final agreement.

6. Backend requirements
Update the AI quote generation context to include:
- service address
- business low-end job cost
- business average job cost
- business high-end job cost
- minimum job charge
- selected quote template
- enabled quote sections
- quote disclaimer
- finalized agent preferences

Add validation so quotes cannot be generated without:
- client_id
- customer request
- service address
- customer contact info
- business pricing context

7. Testing
Add console/server logs for:
- clientId received
- business pricing range loaded
- dynamic budget ranges generated
- selected quote template loaded
- service address received
- quote disclaimer applied
- quote email sent
