Refactor Business Profile into a 6-part structured onboarding flow.

The BDA Setup Helper should be a small card at the top of the Business Profile page, not the main interface. It should say:
“Need help? I can help with questions about a filed we capture, write policies, estimate rules, and customer-facing language after you enter the basics.”

The main interface should be structured forms, not a large AI chat.

Create 6 moving steps:

1. Basic Business Info
2. Company Operations
3. Services Offered
4. Pricing & Rates
5. Policies & Estimate Rules
6. Business Tone & Review

Each step should have Save & Continue, Back, and progress indicators.

Use proper field types, validation, and database columns. Avoid dumping everything into one unstructured text field.

AI Helper behavior:
The BDA Setup Helper should be small and contextual. It should not ask every question at once. It should only help write policy language, estimate rules, tone, and missing-field suggestions.

Validation:
- Currency fields must be decimals
- Percent fields must be decimals
- Employee count and years in business must be whole numbers
- Required fields should show validation errors
- Phone/email/website should validate format
- Text fields should enforce max lengths
- Do not allow raw HTML/script input
